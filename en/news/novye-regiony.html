Expanding our delivery map

June 3, 2026

Good news for our partners in the regions — the GULTRADE delivery zone has grown again.

We now bring orders to the Akmola, Karaganda, Kostanay and Pavlodar regions, as well as East and North Kazakhstan. The schedule is built so flowers reach you the day the truck arrives from Europe or the next morning.

If your city is not on the list yet, write to us: routes are shaped by partner demand, and we plan the next expansion based on your requests.
