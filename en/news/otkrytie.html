GULTRADE opens for business

March 15, 2026

We are delighted to announce: GULTRADE is officially open for business in Kazakhstan!

Our office and warehouse have opened in Astana, and the first truck of fresh-cut flowers has already arrived from a European auction. Florists and flower shops in Kazakhstan can now buy directly — without long chains of middlemen.

What we offer our partners from day one:

  • weekly deliveries of cut flowers and potted plants from the Netherlands, Germany and Denmark;
  • a personal manager who guides your order from request to delivery;
  • related goods — packaging, accessories and planters — in a single order.

Come and meet us at the office or message us on WhatsApp — let's discuss your first order.
