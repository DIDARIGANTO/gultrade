Why partners enjoy working with us

Partners trust us because we are conscientious in every delivery: we answer for quality, keep deadlines, and our streamlined logistics and minimal costs mean the best prices

Uninterrupted supply

Trucks arrive from Europe every week. Your order comes on time — regardless of season, holidays or logistics load.

Wide assortment

All kinds of cut flowers, potted and garden plants, bonsai and exotics. If something is not in stock, we will source it to order.

Buying directly from growers

Once your request is in, the manager finds and buys plants to your specification — no extra middlemen or markups.

Office in Astana

Our office team will advise on the assortment, help pick the right goods and solve any issue on the spot.

Wholesale only — no retail

We have no retail outlets of our own, so we never compete with partners or dump prices.

One-stop-shop principle

Cut flowers, potted plants, packaging, accessories and planters — everything your shop needs in a single order.

A team of professionals
